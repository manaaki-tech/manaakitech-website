About Manaaki Tech

Based in New Zealand, we are a small team building technology that honours and supports the communities we work with. Our name, Manaaki, reflects our core values: hospitality, care, and respect for all people.

Manaaki Central is our first step: helping social services connect with and support whānau, with cultural values built into how it works rather than added on afterwards.
