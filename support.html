We take security seriously, and these are the measures in place:

  • End-to-end encryption for all data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Role-based access controls
  • Compliance with relevant data protection regulations
  • Regular backups and disaster recovery procedures

Our Enterprise plan also offers additional security features including on-premises deployment options for organisations with specific security requirements.
