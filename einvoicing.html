Does This Apply To You?

E-invoicing applies to every person conducting business in the UAE, whether or not you are registered for VAT, unless you or the transaction is specifically excluded.

In scope now

  • Business-to-business (B2B) transactions
  • Business-to-government (B2G) transactions

Outside the mandate for now

  • Business-to-consumer (B2C) transactions. A business that deals only with consumers is not required to comply until the Minister of Finance issues a further decision.
  • Financial services that are VAT-exempt or zero-rated
  • Sovereign activities of government entities that do not compete with the private sector
  • International passenger transport supported by electronic tickets, and related airline services
  • International goods transport supported by an air waybill, for a temporary period of 24 months

A business that is excluded can still choose to adopt e-invoicing voluntarily. If it does, it must follow the rules but is not subject to the administrative penalties.

What The Law Requires

These are the mandatory requirements for businesses within scope.

1

Appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP).

You cannot connect to the government system directly. Every in-scope business must appoint a provider accredited by the Ministry of Finance, and must complete onboarding through the Federal Tax Authority's EmaraTax portal.

2

Issue structured e-invoices, not PDFs.

Invoices and credit notes must be issued in a structured electronic format (XML or JSON) that follows the UAE version of the Peppol standard, known as PINT AE. A PDF or paper invoice is no longer a valid tax invoice for in-scope transactions.

3

Capture every mandatory data field.

Each e-invoice and e-credit note must carry all of the data fields prescribed by the Ministry of Finance, which is fields in the UAE data dictionary. This includes seller and buyer Tax Registration Numbers, a unique invoice identifier, the issue date and time, the tax breakdown, and the invoice type. An invoice with missing or incorrect fields is rejected by the provider and must be corrected and resubmitted.

4

Use the five-corner exchange model.

The invoice moves from your system to your provider, across the Peppol network to your customer's provider, and on to your customer. Your provider also reports the required tax data to the Federal Tax Authority. You do not send invoices to the Authority yourself.

5

Issue and transmit on time.

E-invoices and e-credit notes must be issued and transmitted through the system within your VAT timelines, and in all cases within of the date of the transaction.

6

Treat credit notes the same way.

Credit notes must also be issued electronically, through the same system, with the same data discipline.

7

Store your data in the UAE.

Invoice and credit note data must be stored within the UAE in a way that preserves integrity and allows the Federal Tax Authority to retrieve it.

8

Report system failures within .

If a system failure stops you from complying, you must notify the Federal Tax Authority within .

9

Keep your registered details current.

If your registered business data changes, your provider must be told within the prescribed time. Master data is now a compliance matter, not an administrative one.

What Non-Compliance Costs

Administrative penalties are set by Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025 and apply to businesses within the mandatory scope.

Failure Penalty
Not appointing an ASP and implementing e-invoicing on time
Not issuing and transmitting an e-invoice on time
Not issuing and transmitting an e-credit note on time
Not notifying the Federal Tax Authority of a system failure
Not telling your provider about changes to registered data

These penalties can apply together. The larger cost is usually operational: a rejected invoice is a delayed payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this apply if I am not VAT registered?

Yes, it can. The mandate applies to persons conducting business in the UAE, not only to VAT registrants, subject to the exclusions above.

Our customers are mostly consumers. Do we need to do anything?

Business-to-consumer transactions are outside the mandate for now. If you also sell to other businesses or to government, those sales are in scope. Your consumer sales still count towards your revenue when working out your group.

Can I keep sending PDF invoices?

For in-scope transactions, no. A PDF or paper invoice is not a valid e-invoice. You may still share a readable copy with your customer in addition to the structured e-invoice.

Can I submit invoices directly to the Federal Tax Authority?

No. Invoices must go through an Accredited Service Provider, which validates them and reports the required data to the Authority.

We have missed the appointment date. What now?

Speak to us immediately. Delay attracts a monthly penalty, so the priority is to appoint a provider and onboard as quickly as a sound decision allows. Do not choose a provider only because it is the first on the list.
